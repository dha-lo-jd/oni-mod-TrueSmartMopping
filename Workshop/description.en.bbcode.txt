[h1]"TRUE" Smart Mopping[/h1]

Polluted water trickles in, bit by bit, and your dupes, ever so dutiful, keep mopping it up, 37 grams at a time...

Enough with the tedious, high-maintenance micromanagement of every single spill! Oh, and look: someone dropped some ice, and now it's melting all over your living quarters!

It sounds like it's time for [b]"TRUE" Smart Mopping.[/b]

[h2]✨ Smart Mopping[/h2]

"TRUE" Smart Mopping introduces two key features.

The first feature is [b]"Smart Mopping."[/b]

Instead of the standard mopping command, use the [b]Smart Mopping[/b] tool. No need for fussy area selection: just click anywhere on the puddle, and the game will automatically place the task in the most efficient spot, mopping up the liquid from its edges.

Then, just sit back and wait. [i]Scourgify![/i]

Surprisingly, Smart Mopping doesn't cheat or artificially boost your dupes' mopping speed: each dupe mops at exactly the speed of the standard mopping task. What it does is stand them where a very careful player would. It takes the thick parts first, then dries what is left from the edge of the puddle, so no thin film is left behind, and the task only ends when every drop is gone.

Up to two more dupes can join the task. Two or more dupes working one puddle take turns drying it from one end, a cell apart: one holds back the flow while the other dries the cell ahead of it. That is the fastest pattern of the base-game command, and Smart Mopping simply sets it up for you.

If you're exceptionally diligent, and willing to sacrifice your precious time and focus, you could technically achieve the same results using only the base-game mopping commands.

[h2]✨ Auto-Mopping[/h2]

The second feature is [b]"Auto-Mopping."[/b]

Ever found your pristine living quarters suddenly flooded with liquid?

Auto-Mopping is here to protect both your sanity and your base.

Whenever a dupe comes into contact with a liquid, an auto-mop task is triggered: specifically, a [b][i]Smart Mopping[/i][/b] task.

Of course, I'm well aware that many of you use liquid airlocks to maintain a vacuum or spill small amounts of liquid to cool down your buildings.

To keep Auto-Mopping from becoming a [i]"liquid airlock eater,"[/i] I've included an option to designate [b]"no-mop" zones.[/b] Drag to define a region and create a no-mop zone for that area; drag again inside it to remove it.

You can configure these settings in the [b]Auto Mopping[/b] overlay.

In this overlay, you can also:
[list]
[*] Turn auto-mopping on or off.
[*] Filter the types of liquids to be automatically mopped up.
[*] Adjust the priority for auto-mopping tasks.
[*] Clear all no-mop zones at once.
[/list]

If a dupe is already assigned a task with a higher priority than auto-mopping, no auto-mop task will be triggered when they step in liquid on their way to that task.

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*] English and Japanese.
[/list]
